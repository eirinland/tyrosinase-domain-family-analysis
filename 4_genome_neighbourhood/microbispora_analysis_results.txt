7. ARGUMENTS FOR PSEUDOENZYME STATUS
=====================================
Pseudoenzymes: catalytically inactive enzyme-family members with non-catalytic
functions. ~5-10% of enzyme family members are pseudoenzymes (Ribeiro et al.
2019, Science Signaling 12:eaat9797).

Known pseudoenzyme functions:
  - Allosteric activation of canonical enzymes
  - Scaffolding for protein complex assembly
  - Molecular switches in signaling
  - Substrate/enzyme sequestration
  - Some retain unexpected non-canonical enzymatic activities

This Microbispora protein fits the pseudoenzyme pattern:
  - Homologous to tyrosinase family (PF00264 domain)
  - Lacks all catalytic residues for Cu-dependent oxidase activity
  - Maintains structural fold and is under purifying selection
  - The conserved substitution pattern (not random loss) suggests the
    remodeled active site is itself functional


8. WHAT THE PROTEIN IS NOT
============================
- Not melanin biosynthesis: no melC1 copper chaperone, no melanin pathway genes
- Not in a biosynthetic gene cluster: no PKS/NRPS nearby
- Not a Cu-dependent oxidase: crystal-confirmed no copper
- Not a pseudogene: too well-conserved, actively exported


9. CLUES TOWARD FUNCTION
==========================
- TAT export -> functions outside cytoplasm (periplasm or extracellular)
- TYR replacing CuA_His1 puts a phenol OH inside the active site pocket
  (tyrosinases act ON phenols; this has a phenol IN the active site)
- TYR + GLN + HIS in the pocket could create H-bond network for binding
- Flanking genes are stress-response/housekeeping (OsmC, MsrB, GlgX)
  -- likely synteny, not functional partnership
- Precedent: type-3 Cu proteins evolved enzyme->carrier (hemocyanin)
  multiple times (Aguilera et al. 2013, BMC Evol Biol 13:96)
